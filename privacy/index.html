Privacy Policy

This policy explains information handled by Puzzle Picnic, its advertising and download services, and this support site.

App operator: 김재엽 (Mellow Picnic Studio)
Effective: 2026-10-20

1. Your game stays on your device

Puzzle Picnic has no player account, sign-in, chat, in-app purchases or user-uploaded content. Puzzle completion records, best move counts, an unfinished puzzle in progress and your sound and vibration preferences are stored locally. The game does not provide its own cloud-save service or send these records to a developer-run progress server.

Downloaded puzzle files are stored on the device. Apple's backup, restore and storage services are governed by your device settings and Apple's policies.

2. Ads and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob to show interstitial ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent and privacy choices. Ads may appear when you use a hint.

Google's SDKs may process an IP address and approximate location, app- or device-related identifiers, ads viewed and interactions with ads or the app, crash information, performance measurements and other diagnostics. These data support ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, SDK operation and consent management.

The app requests non-personalized ads, disables Google's publisher first-party ID and does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing; for example, Google may use identifiers or similar storage for frequency limits, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention. These settings do not mean that no information is collected.

When Google's consent flow requires a privacy message, the game presents it before requesting ads. You can revisit choices through Privacy settings in the game's Settings when that action is available. Google's handling of information is described in its privacy policy and advertising documentation.

3. Downloads supplied by Apple

The first 50 puzzle pictures are included in the app. The other 707 pictures are delivered in Apple-hosted asset packs as you play or when you choose Download all puzzles. Apple processes the network and service information needed to provide these downloads under its own privacy policy. Downloading a picture does not create a Puzzle Picnic account.

  • Apple Privacy Policy

4. Support messages and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate reported problems. Please send only what is needed; do not include passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal details.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations. You may contact us to ask about or request deletion of information you sent. Any information that must be retained for an applicable obligation may need to be kept.

This website has no account, contact form or added analytics or advertising scripts. Its hosting service receives connection information, such as your IP address and browser request, to deliver and protect the site. Links to Google, Apple and your email app open services governed by their respective policies.

5. Your choices and questions

Use the game's available Privacy settings to review consent choices. You can remove local game data by deleting the app; copies in device backups are managed separately through Apple. You can also manage downloaded content and device storage through iOS.

For questions about this policy or information you sent to support, use the contact address below. Google and Apple process data through their services, and may do so in countries other than yours; their policies explain their practices and available privacy controls.
