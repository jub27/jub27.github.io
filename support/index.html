How do I solve a puzzle?

Drag a picture block up, down, left or right. Blocks slide only through empty cells; they never rotate or jump over each other. Put every block back into the picture frame to complete the illustration. The two workbench rows below the frame are space for moving blocks out of the way. Empty cells in the frame show a faint copy of the finished picture, and the counter below the board shows your moves next to the shortest solution.

Why won't a block move?

A block stops at the first occupied cell or at the edge of the board, and a slide that cannot complete returns when you let go. Move another block first. Every puzzle needs at least one block, sometimes one already in its place, to step aside and make room.

What do Undo, Hint and Reset do?

Undo reverses your most recent slide. When you choose Hint (marked with AD), an available ad may appear and the board previews one legal next slide at its destination, so you can make the move yourself. Press and hold Reset to return the puzzle to the same starting arrangement.

What is Replay moves?

After finishing a puzzle, choose Replay moves to watch the slides you made, including Undo. Playback does not complete the puzzle again.

How do I get the next puzzle?

Puzzles open in order. Finish the current puzzle to unlock the next one. You can replay earlier puzzles. In the collection, the double-chevron button scrolls to your first unfinished puzzle.

A puzzle image isn't downloading

The first 50 pictures are included in the app. Later pictures download from Apple as needed. Check your connection and available device storage, then tap the puzzle card again. Settings also has Download all puzzles, with stop, resume and retry actions. A download already requested from iOS may finish after you stop the queue.

Can I play offline?

Included pictures and pictures already downloaded can be played offline while their files remain on the device. A new picture needs an internet connection if it hasn't been downloaded yet.

Where is my progress saved?

Completed puzzles and the sound setting are saved on this device. Puzzle Picnic does not provide an account or its own cloud-save service. Deleting the app can remove its progress. Apple's device backups and restores are controlled separately by your iOS settings.

Why do ads appear?

The game is free and includes ads. An available ad may appear when you use a Hint (marked with AD). Completing puzzles without using a hint does not show an ad. If an ad fails to close or interrupts play, tell us what happened without clicking the ad.

How do I change sound, language or privacy choices?

Use Settings to turn effect sounds or vibration on or off. The game follows your iPhone's language preferences, including its per-app language setting, and supports Korean, English, Japanese, German, French, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. A Privacy settings action appears in the game when Google's consent flow requires it.
